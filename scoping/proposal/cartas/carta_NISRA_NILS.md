**Subject:** Feasibility enquiry: early-childhood exposure to the Troubles (1971-76) and adult outcomes using the Northern Ireland Longitudinal Study

Dear NILS-RSU team,

I am a PhD candidate in Economics at the [University of Alicante], Spain, supervised by [supervisor's name]. My dissertation studies how the age at which a child is exposed to a bounded episode of political violence (in utero, 0-2, 3-5, school age) shapes mental health, education and employment in adulthood. Northern Ireland is one of my two study cases: conflict deaths rose roughly eighteen-fold between 1970 and 1972, stayed at their peak until 1976 and then fell to a long, much lower plateau, and the violence was concentrated in a small number of wards. To my knowledge, NILS is the only dataset in which the cohorts born 1964-1982 can be located in childhood and followed to objective adult mental-health outcomes, and no study has yet examined early-childhood exposure by cohort and ward.

The design compares, within ward and within cohort, NILS members born 1964-1982 whose early years overlapped the 1971-76 peak to different degrees. Exposure would be built from the Sutton/McKeown death records geocoded to 1981 wards (or a 1 km grid) and supplied by me as an area-level file. Outcomes would be qualifications and economic activity (1991-2021 censuses), the self-reported mental-health condition (2011 and 2021), mortality, and, through a Distinct Linkage Project, psychotropic prescribing from the Enhanced Prescribing Database. Cohorts born 1977-1982 would serve as a placebo group.

Before preparing a full application I would be grateful for your guidance on the following points:

1. **1981 Census location.** For members enumerated as children in 1981, is ward of residence available (and on which boundary set), and is a finer unit (enumeration district or grid reference) held? What is the 1981 Census linkage rate for these cohorts, and is there documentation on the 1981 undercount in areas affected by the enumeration boycott?
2. **Birth registrations from 1974.** Does the linked birth record carry the mother's usual address at the time of birth (postcode, ward or grid reference), or only the place of registration or occurrence? Is it available for all NILS members born from 1974 onwards?
3. **Census mental-health items.** Could you confirm the exact wording and coding of the 2011 and 2021 items on an "emotional, psychological or mental health condition" within the long-term conditions question, and that both are in the NILS extract?
4. **Enhanced Prescribing Database.** From which date is EPD linkage available (I understand January 2010), at what granularity (BNF chapter or section, monthly counts), and is a DLP for psychotropic prescribing (BNF chapter 4) feasible for a project of this kind? What are typical DLP timelines?
5. **Migration and attrition.** Are Health Card registration events (address changes, de-registration on emigration) available to identify members who left Northern Ireland, and from what date?
6. **Timelines.** What elapsed time should I expect from submission to data access for (a) a census-and-mortality project and (b) a project that adds a DLP?
7. **Remote access.** Can NILS data be accessed other than in person at the NISRA Secure Environment in Belfast, for example through SafePods, an ONS Secure Research Service connection or other remote arrangements?
